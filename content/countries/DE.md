heading: Hiring from Germany
---
As an IT Specialist, I do not require traditional visa sponsorship or a Labor Market Test. An employment contract meeting the legal salary threshold and an online-filed **Erklärung zum Beschäftigungsverhältnis** would be sufficient to process my own fast-track Blue Card visa at the German Embassy Riyadh, with no cost required from you as an employer or recruiter.

Also eligible for the **Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)**, which is easily transferable to a Blue Card. Credentials are **fully recognized (Anabin H+)**.
